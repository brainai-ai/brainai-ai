# Brain.ai

**One person. Many experiences. Shared learning.**

Brain.ai is pursuing a personal form of general intelligence: a computational counterpart that reflects how an individual thinks and behaves, develops capabilities on their behalf, and brings useful learning back to them.

A person's time is finite. The opportunity is to expand what they can learn and accomplish beyond the experiences they can undertake alone.

We envision one personal intelligence learning and acting across many environments at once, from digital systems toward physical embodiment. The value would extend beyond completed work: knowledge and skills acquired through those experiences should remain useful to the person as their needs and ambitions change.

Our focus is the continuity between the individual, the intelligence that represents them, and what it learns. The aim is not simply to reproduce a person's existing abilities, but to extend their capacity while preserving their authority over what acts in their name.

This is a long-term research commitment. The full ambition extends across generations. The work begins today, with progress judged by demonstrated capability.
